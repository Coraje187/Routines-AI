## 🌟 What's New in v1.0.1

### 📱 1. Responsive Phone Support (Portrait + Landscape)
* **Natural Orientation**: Hold your phone in **Portrait** or rotate to **Landscape** (`fullSensor`).
* **Adaptive Single-Pane UI**: On phones, the app automatically switches to a unified full-width screen with scrollable navigation tabs.
* **`🤖 AI Brain` Tab**: Directly accessible from the tab bar on mobile phones.
* **Swipeable Kid Profile Strip**: Swiping across kid chips is smooth and nothing gets clipped or crowded on smaller screens.
* **Notch & Gesture Bar Padding**: Safe-window insets protect against camera cutouts and bottom gesture pills.

### 🎨 2. Fully Round App Logo
* **Circular Android App Icon**: Replaced squircle with a 100% circular Genesis OS emblem across all density mipmaps (`mdpi` to `xxxhdpi`).
* **No More Clipped Corners**: Fits round-icon launchers (Samsung One UI, Pixel, Motorola) and tablet docks.
* **Stadium Pill In-App Badge**: Smooth, circular branding badge in the top header.
